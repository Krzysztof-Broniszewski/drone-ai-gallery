STAGE 01 — COMFYUI

Product Design & Consistency

The BLACK LUXURY bottle was designed as a standalone product image on a transparent background. A multi-angle character sheet was then generated to preserve the same bottle, cap, materials and branding across later shots.

PRODUCTION SETUP

Tools & Local Hardware

All AI image, video and audio generation was performed locally in ComfyUI on an Intel Core i5-13400 workstation with 64 GB RAM and an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 (24 GB VRAM). Product identity was developed with Qwen Image 2.1 Character Sheet, moving footage with MiniMax H3 First Frame / Last Frame, original soundtrack music with ACE-Step 1.5 Text-to-Music, and cinematic sound effects with Stable Audio 3 Text-to-Audio. Final picture editing, sound layering, synchronization and export were completed in DaVinci Resolve. No cloud-based AI generation service was used.
